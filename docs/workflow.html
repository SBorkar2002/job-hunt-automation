Auto-apply, review in the Sheet

How one run works

Every hour the script goes through Instahyre, Cutshort, Wellfound and LinkedIn, applies to fresh jobs that fit, and writes each one to your Sheet. You don't approve anything. You review the Sheet and tell this chat what to change.
