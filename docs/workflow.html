How one run works

Each site runs on its own every 3 hours, at 8am, 11am, 2pm, 5pm, 8pm and 11pm: Instahyre on the hour, Wellfound at half past. The bot applies to fresh jobs that fit and writes each one to your Sheet. You review the Sheet and tell this chat what to change.

Per site

What's different on each site

Instahyre Every 3 hours · 10 a day

Reads your recommended jobs. "Apply now" is one click and sends your Instahyre profile resume, no note. Applied shows as "Application sent!". No post dates, so no freshness rule.

Wellfound Every 3 hours at :30 · 10 a day

Reads your saved search (Product Designer, Bengaluru, full time). Jobs from the last 14 days. Sends your Wellfound profile plus a note to "What interests you about this company?", which you approve here first. Applied shows as "✓ Applied". Login is Google, done in a normal Chrome window.

Cutshort, Naukri, others Not built

Each new site goes through the same steps: terms and your OK, profile check, recon, filter check with you, one live application, then the timer.

LinkedIn Skipped

Not automated: LinkedIn bans accounts that do this.
