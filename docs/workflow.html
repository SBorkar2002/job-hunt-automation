Sejal's job hunt automation

Job hunt workflow

How a fresh job goes from a site to your Sheet, your phone and an application. It covers LinkedIn, Instahyre, Cutshort and Wellfound.

The four places things happen

Google cloudThe Apps Script. Runs on a timer even with your laptop off. Finds jobs, filters them, writes the Sheet, sends pings.
Telegram on your phoneWhere the system talks to you and you answer with one tap.
YouDecide what to apply to. Apply by hand on LinkedIn.
Your Mac, when awakeUses its own Chrome profile you logged into. Scans logged-in pages and submits approved applications.

When Telegram is used

Telegram is the only way the system can reach you right away and get a one-tap answer back. Without it you would have to keep opening the Sheet to check for new jobs, and nothing could wait for your approval.
