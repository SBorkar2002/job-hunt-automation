Google cloud

LinkedIn finder

Looks at LinkedIn's public job search for the past hour, newest first. It never logs in, so your account is never touched.

Google cloud

Email finder

Your Mac

Logged-in finder

If your Mac is awake, it also checks Instahyre, Cutshort and Wellfound in its own Chrome profile, which you logged into yourself. This catches jobs the emails miss.

In short

Why Telegram is needed

Telegram

It's the only way the system can reach you instantly and get a one-tap answer back. Without it you'd have to keep opening the Sheet to check, and nothing could wait for your approval. It's used for 7 things: new LinkedIn job, new job on the other sites, applied, needs you, Mac asleep, follow-up, and the daily summary.
